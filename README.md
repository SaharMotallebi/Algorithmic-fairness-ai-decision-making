
# Algorithmic Fairness in AI Decision-Making

**Author:** Sahar Motallebi  
**Institution:** Linnaeus University, Sweden  
**Course:** 4IK509  
**Project type:** Individual academic research proposal  
**Field:** Information Systems, Responsible AI and Algorithmic Governance

## Overview

This research proposal examines algorithmic fairness in AI-assisted financial lending, focusing on bias detection, mitigation strategies, regulatory requirements and organizational accountability.

## Research Questions

1. How are bias detection and mitigation strategies addressed in AI-driven lending systems?
2. What challenges hinder the standardization and implementation of algorithmic fairness?
3. How might an information systems-based conceptual framework support more consistent fairness auditing?

## Proposed Methodology

The proposal outlines a systematic literature review approach to examining algorithmic fairness methods, regulatory frameworks and organizational challenges associated with automated lending decisions.

## Expected Contribution

The proposed research seeks to connect technical fairness assessment with regulatory requirements and organizational accountability.

## Individual Contribution

Sole author of the research proposal, including research question formulation, literature analysis, proposed methodology and conceptual research approach.

## Project Status

Completed university coursework. This is a research proposal, not a completed empirical investigation or software implementation.
